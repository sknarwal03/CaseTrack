Administration overview

Police Station creation, Operator provisioning, and Users listing use trusted Firebase Functions. The Functions endpoints verify the active Admin role and validate each Operator station assignment server-side. Admins can also promote, demote, revoke, and reactivate accounts through the role-transition endpoint.

Role changes are server-authorized, recorded in role history, and protected by last-Admin governance. Station IDs remain stable; station rename and deletion are not supported.
